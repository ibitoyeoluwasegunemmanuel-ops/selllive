Privacy Policy

SellLive Nigeria · Last updated: April 7, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account (name, phone number), information generated when you use the app (orders, messages, stream activity), and device information for push notifications.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process orders and payments via Flutterwave
  • To send order updates via WhatsApp and push notifications
  • To connect buyers and sellers through live streams and direct messages
  • To improve our platform and prevent fraud

Payments

All payments are processed by Flutterwave. We do not store your card details. Flutterwave's privacy policy applies to payment data.

Camera and Microphone

We request camera and microphone access only when you choose to go live or join a video call. This data is transmitted via Daily.co and is not stored by SellLive.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate SellLive (Flutterwave, Daily.co, GIG Logistics, Firebase, Termii).

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion by emailing support@selllive.ng.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@selllive.ng.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@selllive.ng
