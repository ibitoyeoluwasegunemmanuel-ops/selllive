🔴 **SellLive — Nigeria's Live Commerce Platform**

Watch Nigerian sellers go live from Lagos markets, type BUY to order in real time, and pay securely with Flutterwave — all from your phone.

**FOR BUYERS:**

📺 **Watch Live Streams**
Sellers from Balogun Market, Computer Village, and across Lagos go live daily. See real products, ask questions, and order instantly.

💬 **Type BUY to Order**
No complicated checkout. Just type "BUY" in the chat while watching a live stream. Your order is created instantly and a payment link is sent.

💳 **Pay with Flutterwave**
Support for cards, bank transfers, USSD, and mobile money. 100% Naira payments. Secured and encrypted.

📦 **Track Your Delivery**
Real-time updates via WhatsApp and in-app notifications. Powered by GIG Logistics and Kwik Delivery.

🛡️ **Buyer Protection**
Your money is held securely until you confirm delivery. Open a dispute if anything goes wrong — we'll mediate within 24 hours.

📱 **Shop Feed**
Scroll through product posts like TikTok or Instagram. Double-tap to like, tap to buy, share on WhatsApp.

💬 **Chat with Sellers**
Direct message, voice call, or video call any seller. See the product up close before you order.

⚡ **Flash Sales**
Time-limited deals with live countdown timers. Sellers offer up to 70% off for a limited time — don't miss out.

🏆 **Loyalty Points**
Earn 1 point for every ₦100 you spend. Redeem 100 points for ₦500 off your next order.

---

**FOR SELLERS:**

🎥 **Go Live in Seconds**
Open the app, tap Go Live, and start showing your products. No studio needed — just your phone.

🛒 **Sell While You Sleep**
Post product videos and photos to your Shop Feed. Buyers can order 24/7, even when you're not live.

💰 **Get Paid Instantly**
Payments hit your SellLive wallet immediately after delivery confirmation. Withdraw to any Nigerian bank account within 24 hours.

📊 **Track Everything**
Analytics dashboard shows revenue, orders, viewer counts, post engagement, and earnings — all in one place.

✅ **Get Verified**
Submit your ID for verification and get a blue badge. Verified sellers earn 3x more buyer trust.

🎁 **Referral Rewards**
Earn ₦500 for every seller you invite to SellLive when they make their first sale.
